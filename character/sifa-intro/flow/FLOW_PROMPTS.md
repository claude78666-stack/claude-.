# Sifa intro: Google Flow shot list

Make these in Google Flow (labs.google/flow) with the Veo 3.1 model, 16:9, 8 s per clip.
Upload `../start.png` and `../seated_end.png` from this folder as frames or ingredients.

## Shot 1: walk to the chair and sit (Frames to Video)
- First frame: `start.png`
- Last frame: `seated_end.png`
- Prompt:
  > Cinematic, photorealistic. A young woman with long loose blonde hair, in a black gamer hoodie and joggers, walks naturally across a colourful RGB-lit gaming room to a gaming chair, turns around and sits down smoothly. Real human motion: natural arm swing, weight shift and bending knees as she sits. Slow camera dolly-in, shallow depth of field, purple and cyan neon light. No text. Ambient room sound, soft footsteps.

## Shot 2: she says hello (Frames to Video, first frame only)
- First frame: `seated_end.png` (or the last frame of Shot 1, using Extend)
- Prompt:
  > Same woman sitting in the gaming chair looks into the camera, smiles and waves with her right hand, then speaks warmly and clearly in a friendly young female voice: "Hi, I'm Sifa, the first S I agent! Today we're jumping into Gears of War: E-Day." Natural lip sync, subtle head movement, photorealistic, neon gaming room, medium close-up.

## Shot 3: what's in today's video (Extend from Shot 2)
- Prompt:
  > She keeps talking to the camera with natural hand gestures, same voice: "The brand new prequel where Marcus Fenix and Dom fight through the very first day of the Locust invasion. I'll play the campaign with you and give you my honest verdict at the end."

## Shot 4: let's go (Extend from Shot 3)
- Prompt:
  > She grins, grabs a game controller from the desk and says excitedly in the same voice: "Let's get started!" Then she turns toward the monitor as the camera pushes in on the glowing screen.

- Use the same Flow project and the "Extend" button so her face, outfit and voice stay consistent.
- Do not use Runway.
