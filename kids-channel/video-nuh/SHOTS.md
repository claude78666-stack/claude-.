# Prophet Nuh and the Great Ark: shot list and prompts

Workflow: **Blender previz** (`previz/`) → **Nano Banana** keyframe image per shot → **Veo / Flow** turns each image into an 8-second clip → edit to the narration.
Runway is not used anywhere in this project.

## Rules for every prompt

- Prophet Nuh is **never** a person. He is a "tall, soft, faceless figure made only of glowing white light". There is no face, hands, clothes or features.
- Allah is never depicted in any form, and is never given a voice.
- Other people are shown at a distance or from behind, in simple ancient clothes, with no close-up faces.
- Nobody is shown drowning. The flood is waves, rain and the ark.
- Style suffix for every image: *"photorealistic, cinematic, ancient Middle East, soft natural light, 16:9, no text, no logos"*.
