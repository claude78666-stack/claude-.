Design rules

  • Long, loose honey-blonde waves past the shoulders, with a small gold hair clip.
  • Deep-teal tactical jacket with dashed gold stitching, worn open over a black high-neck top.
  • Gold SG patch on her left chest; large SG emblem on the back.
  • Teal earpiece on her right ear, HUD smartwatch on her left wrist.
  • Gold controller keychain hanging from the belt.
  • Black cargo pants and boots with a single gold band.
