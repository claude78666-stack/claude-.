# Clippers — Making Money Clipping Videos

Clipping means taking long videos (podcasts, streams, interviews), cutting out the best
15–60 second moments, making them vertical with captions, and posting them on
TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. You get paid for the views.

## How the money works

| Way to earn | How it pays | Typical rate | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clipping campaigns** (Whop "Content Rewards", Vyro, ClipFarm, creators' Discords) | Creator or brand pays per 1,000 views on clips you post | ~$0.50–$3 per 1K views | Day one, with zero followers |
| **Clip a creator directly** | Monthly retainer or per clip | $5–$50 per clip, $300–$2,000+/month | Once you have a portfolio |
| **Your own clip page** | Platform payouts (YouTube Shorts ad share, TikTok Creator Rewards for videos over 1 min) plus brand deals | Low at first, grows with followers | Long term |

**Start with campaigns.** You don't need followers, they pay on views, and the creator has
already given permission to use their content.

## Step-by-step plan

### Week 1: set up
1. Make **new** accounts for one niche (e.g. `@[creator]clips`) on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
   Warm them up for 2–3 days: scroll and like posts in the niche before you post anything.
2. Join 2–3 clipping campaigns (search "clipping" on whop.com, or look in the creator's Discord).
   Read each one's rules: allowed sources, minimum views, required hashtags or tags, payout cap.
3. Install free tools: **CapCut** (editing + auto captions), and optionally **OpusClip**
   (AI finds moments) and **yt-dlp + ffmpeg** (see `clip.sh` in this folder).

### Daily routine (1–2 hours)
1. **Find moments**: open the newest long video from your campaign creator. Look for
   hot takes, arguments, funny moments, shocking stats, emotional stories and "wait, what?" lines.
2. **Cut 3–5 clips** of 20–45 seconds each. Start on the strongest line, with no intro.
3. **Edit**: 9:16 vertical, big centered captions (2–4 words at a time), punch-in zooms,
   a hook text on screen for the first 2 seconds.
4. **Post** each clip on all 3 platforms, spread through the day.
5. **Submit** the links to the campaign dashboard to get paid.
6. **Track** views in a spreadsheet. Make more of whatever works.

### Hook formulas (text overlay for the first 2 seconds)
- "He didn't expect this answer…"
- "This is why [topic] is broken"
- "[Creator] just exposed [thing]"
- "Nobody talks about this"
- "Wait for the end 😭"

### Caption/title template
`[Hook sentence] #[creator] #[niche] #podcast #fyp` plus any tag the campaign requires.

## Realistic numbers
- 5 clips/day × 3 platforms = 15 posts/day.
- Most clips get 200–2K views. Roughly 1 in 20–50 goes viral (100K+).
- At $1 per 1K views, one 500K-view clip = **$500**. Payouts depend on volume and
  consistency, and most people earn little in the first few weeks.
- Moving up: after ~30 days show your best stats to creators and pitch a retainer
  ("I got your clips 2M views last month — I'll run your clip page for $X/month").

## Rules that keep you safe
- Only clip content you have **permission** for (campaigns, or the creator said yes). Random
  copyrighted clips get strikes, demonetization and banned accounts.
- Don't use fake views, bots or purchased engagement. Campaigns check for it and won't pay.
- Add real editing (captions, cuts, hooks). Re-uploads with no changes get flagged as "unoriginal".
- Watch out for scams: never pay to join a "clipping job".
- Payouts count as self-employment income, so keep records for taxes.

## What I can do vs. what you must do
**I can:** find trending videos and moments, read transcripts and pick timestamps, write hooks,
captions and titles, cut and format clips (`clip.sh`), write pitch messages, and build a tracker.

**You must:** create and log in to the social accounts, sign up for campaigns (they need your
identity and payout details), post the clips, and accept payment. Platforms require a real
person for these steps.
