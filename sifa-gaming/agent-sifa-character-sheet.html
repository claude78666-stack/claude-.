Agent Sifa

"Every level has a secret. Sifa finds it."

Codename: Agent S

Profile

Name
Sifa
Codename
Agent S
Age
Mid-20s
Role
Field agent of the Sifa Gaming division. She finds the secrets, hidden routes and winning strategy in every game.
Personality
Calm and confident on the surface, fiercely competitive underneath. Friendly to the audience.
Signature move
A cool, steady look into the camera, then a quick smirk.

Look

  • Tall and slim with model proportions, about eight heads tall.
  • Shoulder-length platinum-blonde hair, soft side part, loose wisps.
  • A thin braid along her right temple, finished with a small gold clip.
  • Large grey-green eyes with a soft winged lash line.
  • Fair skin, light blush and soft rose lips.

Design rules

  • Open deep-teal tactical jacket with dashed gold stitching over a black high-neck top.
  • Gold SG patch on her left chest; large SG emblem on the back.
  • Teal earpiece on her right ear, HUD smartwatch on her left wrist.
  • Gold controller keychain on the belt. Slim black cargo pants and boots with one gold band.
  • Clean lineart, soft cel shading and warm window light, in the style of a Korean webtoon.
