# Sifa format v2: more engaging, more human

What the most-watched E-Day videos do (checked on YouTube, October 2026), and how Sifa's videos change
because of it.

## What works in this niche

| Channel / video | Views | What it does well |
|---|---|---|
| gameranx, "Gears of War E-Day – Before You Buy" (15:32) | 1.2M in 2 weeks | Gameplay from the first second; the host says who he is and what the video is in one breath; clear segments (mechanics, open zones, story, visuals, verdict); every sentence has matching footage (says "slide", you see a slide); strong opinions in a casual voice; ends by asking viewers a question for the comments before like/subscribe. |
| theRadBrad, "Walkthrough Part 1 – Intro" | 990K | Full campaign parts, consistent naming ("Part 1 – …"), big expressive thumbnail, chapters. |
| Hollow, "Part 1 – WHERE IT ALL BEGAN.." | 1.0M | Emotional episode titles, not just numbers. |
| IGN, "Campaign Review" | 495K | Score and verdict early, then the reasons, quotable lines. |

The reference short (gesture): the host raises her right hand to chest/head height, fingers loosely curled,
and moves it up and down in a "nodding yes" rhythm while landing her point. Word-by-word yellow captions
run under her the whole time.

## The changes

1. **Hook in the first 5–10 s**: fast gameplay montage, slam-in title "GEARS OF WAR: E-DAY – EVERYTHING
   YOU NEED TO KNOW", and a hook line with an opinion: *"E-Day is finally here, and it might be the best
   Gears in fifteen years. Here's everything you need to know before you buy."*
2. **Sifa feels alive**: she waves, talks with her hands (open palms, counting on fingers, pointing at the
   screen), and does the yes-pump only on her key lines (e.g. "the first S-I agent", "this is the best
   Gears in years"), never continuously. She swivels her chair to the monitor and looks at the screen
   before the gameplay starts, and the camera pushes into the screen.
3. **Gameplay always matches the words**, with Sifa's facecam small in the bottom left.
4. **Word-by-word captions** in yellow highlight, all the way through.
5. **Motion graphics**: chapter tag + progress bar, pop-up feature badges ("NEW: JUMP • SLIDE • CLIMB"),
   stat callouts ("14 HOURS", "12 PLAYERS"), punch-in zooms on big moments, whoosh sounds on cuts.
6. **Cutbacks to Sifa** between sections (2–4 s, a gesture and a reaction) so it's not one long voice-over.
7. **Ending with a question** for the comments, then like/subscribe, then the SIFA SI sign-off.

## How Sifa's motion gets made (free tools, never Runway)

- **Preview**: Blender animatic (`previz/`), stand-in Sifa, to sign off staging, gestures and timing.
- **Final photoreal motion**: image-to-video from Sifa's seated keyframe, one short clip per gesture
  (wave, yes-pump, open palms, counting, pointing, chair swivel and look at the screen), then lip-synced
  to her voice with Wav2Lip. Free options: Google Flow / Veo (monthly free credits, made by you in the
  browser), Kling (free daily credits once its connector is signed in), or Hugging Face ZeroGPU Spaces
  (Wan / LTX-Video) once there's a Hugging Face token this session can use.
